## 1 — Brand System

### Brand character

The N27 brand is **classical hospitality, investor-grade**. Think of the typographic vocabulary of a well-established steakhouse, a long-standing architecture firm, or a private bank — restraint, serifs, generous whitespace, a single accent color used sparingly. The visual vocabulary signals that N27 is an operator-led firm with skin in the game, not a pitch-deck factory.

### Palette

A tight monochrome foundation with a single deep lime-green accent. This is the entire palette — resist any urge to add secondary accents.

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black (dominant) | `#0A0A0A` | Full black | Dark slide backgrounds, titles on ivory |
| Ivory | `#F5EEE3` | Warm off-white | Text on black, card fills on dark slides |
| Deep lime (sole accent) | `#4A5D3A` | Muted forest green | Rules, eyebrows, stats, key numbers, markers |
| Lime dark | `#33412A` | Darker lime | Body-weight lime text on ivory |
| Ink | `#1A1A1A` | Near black | Body text on ivory surfaces |
| Mute | `#8A7E6E` | Warm gray | Subtitles, captions, muted details |
| Rule | `#DDD5C7` | Soft ivory-gray | Hairlines, table borders |
| Cream | `#FAF6EE` | Faint ivory | Alternating-row fill in tables |

**Use rule:** Black + ivory carry 90%+ of surface area. Deep lime is the singular accent. Never introduce red, amber, blue, or navy.

### Typography

| Element | Font | Size | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slide title | Cinzel (or Trajan Pro) | 28–32pt | Bold |
| Subtitle | Georgia | 14–15pt | Italic |
| Eyebrow / section tag | Calibri | 10–11pt | Bold, all caps, tracked 60–80 |
| Section header | Cinzel | 20–22pt | Bold |
| Body | Georgia | 12–13pt | Regular |
| Caption / source | Georgia | 9–10pt | Italic, mute |
| Big stat callout | Cinzel | 28–40pt | Bold, deep lime |

**Fallbacks:** If Cinzel isn't in Claude Design's font library, try in this order: Trajan Pro → Playfair Display → Cormorant Garamond → Georgia Bold. All preserve the classical-Roman-capital character. **Do not fall back to Montserrat, Poppins, Inter, or any geometric sans-serif** — it breaks the brand vocabulary entirely.

**Rule:** Keep slide titles at 30pt maximum on LAYOUT_WIDE to avoid wrapping. Long titles split into title + subtitle rather than force-wrap.

### Visual Motifs

Four repeatable design elements that carry the N27 DNA through every piece of collateral:

1. **★ ★ ★ three-star divider.** Three small deep-lime stars, centered, between major sections or above/below key statements. The star is N27's signature mark.
2. **Single centered ★.** Used as a section-open flourish above slide titles on key slides (cover, Ask, Thank You).
3. **Deep-lime hairline.** `#4A5D3A`, 1–2pt, short (1.5–2.5" wide, never full-width). Used under cover title, above signatures, and separating hierarchy within dark panels.
4. **Category tagline lockup.** `REAL ESTATE · RESTAURANTS · MOBILE FOOD` in small tracked italic serif, mute color. Used on cover, Thank You slide, and end-of-document signature block. This is N27's elevator pitch compressed to six words.

### Footer convention

Every content slide (2–13, 16) has a three-column footer:
- Left: `NUMBER 20 SEVEN LLC` (Calibri, 9pt, bold, mute, tracked)
- Center: `CONFIDENTIAL · INVESTOR DECK` (Calibri, 9pt, mute, tracked)
- Right: `NN / 16` (Calibri, 9pt, deep lime)

Above footer: thin `#DDD5C7` hairline spanning full content width.

### Layout Defaults

- Slide size: **LAYOUT_WIDE (13.3" × 7.5")**
- Backgrounds: **white or ivory (`#F5EEE3`)** for content slides; **full black (`#0A0A0A`)** for cover, Ask, Thank You
- Title: `x: 0.5, y: 0.9, w: 12.3, h: 0.7` — max 30pt
- Subtitle: `x: 0.5, y: 1.7, w: 12.3, h: 0.4`
- Content area: starts at `y: 2.4`
- Footer: at `y: 7.1`

### What to avoid

- Any color other than black, ivory, deep lime, and their muted counterparts
- Sans-serif headers (Calibri, Helvetica, Arial) — Cinzel or a classical serif only
- More than three colors in any chart (shades of lime + ivory + black)
- Decorative lines under titles — use star motifs and lime hairlines only, where specified
- Heavy full-width colored banners — the N27 aesthetic is restraint
- Emojis, icons in colored circles, or playful flourishes
- Cream background on content slides — use plain white or ivory (`#F5EEE3`)

Number 20 Seven LLC is a **holding company**. Its first operating subsidiary is a Boston-based brick-and-mortar restaurant that grounds N27's advisory work in live restaurant operations. The food-truck franchise expansion thesis is N27's flagship advisory play, not its only line of business — the brand system supports future verticals (additional restaurant concepts, real-estate plays, other mobile-food initiatives) under the same masthead.

When writing future N27 collateral, the positioning line is:

> **Number 20 Seven LLC — Real Estate · Restaurants · Mobile Food**

That's the tagline. It's the umbrella. Subsidiaries, products, and advisory engagements sit beneath it.
