★ THE PREMISE

Food trucks built the brands of the last decade.
We're flipping the model.

Yesterday's truck became today's chain. Tomorrow's chain should launch trucks — cheaper, faster, and as real-estate reconnaissance.

YESTERDAY · 2010s

Truck → Brick-and-Mortar

★ ★ ★
  • Cousins Maine Lobster grew from one LA truck to >$1B systemwide.
  • Kogi BBQ, Halal Guys, and Roxy's turned food trucks into brick-and-mortar chains.
  • Micro-dining proved that accessibility is its own vertical — not a stepping stone.
TOMORROW · 2026+

Brick-and-Mortar → Truck

★ ★ ★
  • QSR brands can test neighborhoods before signing $1M+ leases.
  • Lower barrier to entry means more operators — same royalty, deeper penetration.
  • Every truck doubles as real-estate reconnaissance for future locations.

★ BRAND SCENARIOS

Three brand archetypes.
Three paths to scale.

We're brand-agnostic by design. The model works across global QSR, fast-casual, and celebrity-backed tiers — each with its own fee structure and rationale.

TIER I
GLOBAL QSR

McDonald's-class

Why they need this.

Real-estate masters with huge brand equity — but capex-heavy brick-and-mortar slows expansion into secondary markets.

Where we plug in.

Market-entry scouts in secondary zip codes; micro-dining formats for urban corridors.

TIER II
FAST CASUAL

Qdoba / Teriyaki Madness

Why they need this.

Already run 5–6% royalty structures and scale through franchisees. Lower-cost expansion is a natural fit.

Where we plug in.

Phase-0 market testing before committing to a 10-year retail lease in a new MSA.

TIER III
CELEBRITY & REGIONAL

Wahlburgers / Tasty Burger

Why they need this.

Brand equity outruns physical footprint. Strong Boston roots, receptive to innovation and local expansion.

Where we plug in.

Triple their trade-area reach without adding leases — or capturing new demographics entirely.
